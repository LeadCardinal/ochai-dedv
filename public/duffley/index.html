What I found before this page existed

Three places Duffley Law is leaving efficiency on the table.

Publicly observable. Operationally fixable. High-ROI from day one.

Intake is running on five humans where one agent could carry the load.

Five dedicated intake staff — 26% of total headcount — manually routing every inbound lead. No chatbot. No self-scheduling. After-hours inquiries hit a static form and wait. At $90K monthly revenue, every lead stalling in the queue is margin walking out the door.

Fix: AI intake agent — qualifies, schedules & routes 24/7

Flat-fee volume + manual drafting = a margin problem that compounds daily.

Flat-fee pricing is the right model for growth. But every estate plan drafted manually is time your attorneys can't bill elsewhere. A firm producing high-volume standardized documents — wills, trusts, POAs, TODDs — is leaving the single biggest AI efficiency gain untouched.

Fix: AI drafting layer — first drafts in minutes, attorney signs off in seconds

Client communication runs on ad-hoc group chats. That doesn't scale to 19 people.

Google reviews reveal clients being coordinated via personal group texts — a workaround, not a system. No client portal, no structured status updates, no automated milestone notifications. At current growth velocity, that breaks publicly before long.

Fix: Automated client comms pipeline — status updates on autopilot
